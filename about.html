About Ozone Fitness Planet

Founded in Kurnool on one belief: real results come from real coaching, not fancy machines. Every program here is led by a trainer who has competed on stage himself.

How We Started

Built On The Platform, Not Behind A Clipboard

Ozone Fitness Planet was founded by Mr. Md. Khan Sab, a competitive bodybuilder with 25 years on the gym floor. Too many members were quitting on generic programs that ignored their actual health conditions — so Ozone was built to do the opposite.

Today, every member gets a plan shaped around their own body: medical history, current condition, and real goals — coached by a team that trains alongside you, not just from behind a desk.
