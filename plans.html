House Rules

Gym Etiquette

What we expect from every member on the floor.

Hygiene & Health

  • Towel mandatory — no towel, no workout.
  • Wipe down machines after every use.
  • Separate clean indoor shoes required; outside shoes stay off the floor.
  • Members with diabetes, PCOS or heart conditions must keep trainers updated on any change in medication or health status.

Equipment Etiquette

  • Re-rack every dumbbell and plate — don't leave weights on the floor.
  • No dropping or slamming weights.
  • Share the space; don't sit on machines scrolling your phone between sets.
  • Notice a loose or broken machine? Report it to a trainer immediately.

Professional Conduct

  • Advanced lifts only under certified trainer supervision.
  • Proper athletic attire — t-shirts and track pants/shorts — required at all times.
  • Respect the privacy and personal space of other members.
  • Keep phone calls short and quiet; use headphones for music.

Safety First

  • Warm up for at least 5–10 minutes before every session.
  • Always ask a trainer for a spot when lifting heavy.
  • Stay hydrated — proper hydration supports blood sugar and metabolic health.
  • No workout is permitted without admission and a base health check.
