Service Breakdown

Tap a service to see what's included and why it matters for your body.

01 Weight / Fat Loss+

Fat loss is the most requested goal at Ozone — and the one most damaged by generic advice. Your program is built around your current condition, activity level, and diet, then adjusted every few weeks based on real progress, not guesswork.

What's Included
  • Body composition check-in
  • Structured training split for fat loss
  • Diet guidance from a certified nutritionist
  • Monthly progress review
02 Muscle Strengthening+

Built for members who want visible, lasting strength — not just a pump. Programming follows progressive overload principles, with form corrected in real time by a trainer, not left to guesswork on your own.

What's Included
  • Progressive resistance programming
  • Free-weight and machine access
  • Form correction from certified trainers
  • Periodic strength benchmarking
03 Flexibility & Endurance+

Mobility work that protects every other goal on this list. Better range of motion and stamina mean fewer injuries, better lifts, and a body that recovers faster between sessions.

What's Included
  • Guided mobility and stretching routines
  • Endurance-building cardio blocks
  • Injury-prevention focused coaching
04 Yoga / Zumba+

A lower-impact complement to strength and cardio days — yoga for breath and recovery, Zumba for a high-energy, music-driven cardio session that doesn't feel like a chore.

What's Included
  • Guided yoga sessions
  • Zumba group classes
  • Recovery-focused breathing work
05 Cross-Fit+

High-intensity, functional training for members chasing all-round conditioning rather than one specific goal. Sessions are trainer-led and scaled to your current fitness level.

What's Included
  • Trainer-led functional circuits
  • Scaled intensity for all levels
  • Included in the Personal Training package (2 sessions/month)
06 Personal Training Most Popular+

Ozone's most complete program. A dedicated trainer works with you every session, with a diet plan built from your medical reports and monthly weight-loss targets tracked closely.

What's Included
  • Approximately 3–4 kg of weight loss per month
  • A dedicated personal trainer with you in every session
  • A diet plan customised to your medical reports
  • 2 cross-fit sessions included
  • Full-body stretching for muscle recovery
07 Small Group Training+

For members who want personal-training style attention split with a friend or small crew — more affordable, still closely coached, and often more motivating with company on the floor.

What's Included
  • Shared coaching for 2 or more people
  • Same trainer-led structure as solo PT
08 Fitness Assessment / Orientation+

Every new member starts here — no workout is permitted without an orientation and base health check, so your program starts from an accurate picture of where you are today.

What's Included
  • Baseline fitness assessment
  • Gym orientation and equipment walkthrough
  • Health and medical disclosure review
